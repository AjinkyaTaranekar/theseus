# E2: IAM: statements, matching, CAN and CAN_ASSUME

**Lane:** Engine | **Stage:** Beat 1: See it | **Size:** L | **Needs first:** F2 | **Branch:** `e2-iam`

## The feature

The IAM subset as pure functions: collect each principal's statements (inline, attached customer-managed, the AWS-managed table), evaluate them, and derive `CAN` and `CAN_ASSUME` edges, including DynamoDB actions and S3 bucket policies for `entry:internet`.

## Why it matters

Whether a path exists at all is decided here. Managed policies are how most real Terraform grants access; UNKNOWN instead of a guess is one of our strongest answers to judges.
