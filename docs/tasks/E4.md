**Lane:** Engine | **Stage:** Beat 2: Fix it | **Size:** M | **Needs first:** E3 | **Branch:** `e4-min-cut`

## The feature

Capacities from the requirements, `algo.maxFlow` in FalkorDB, the cut derived from the residual graph, an Edmonds-Karp fallback in Python, and `min_cut` in every PR result.

## Why it matters

"The math rules out deleting a permission" (intern) and "one edge carries no required access" (Beat 3) both come from here. It sits on the critical path, so it stays small: betweenness is FM4's.
