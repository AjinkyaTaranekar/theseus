# A2: Jev: sensitivity labels and advisory urgency

**Lane:** Agent and GitHub | **Stage:** Beat 1: See it | **Size:** M | **Needs first:** F2 | **Branch:** `a2-jev`

## The feature

The Jev client with record and replay and a Claude fallback; sensitivity labels for every resource node; and the urgency question for an advisory, recorded now and used by finding mode.

## Why it matters

Sensitivity decides which paths are dangerous. In the PoC Jev rated 30 of 30 names correctly in about a third of a second. The urgency score is System 1 making a fast typed call in Beat 3.
