# A3: Says versus grants

**Lane:** Agent and GitHub | **Stage:** Beat 1: See it | **Size:** S | **Needs first:** E3, A1 | **Branch:** `a3-says-vs-grants`

## The feature

Intent becomes requirements in the pipeline, and the result gets its headline: what the PR says against what the change grants. Words explain a change but never approve it: intent requirements on sensitive data, or from a suspicious PR, are unapproved.

## Why it matters

This is the opening line of the video: "Your PR says logs. Your change grants the customer database."
