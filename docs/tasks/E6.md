# E6: Rule-based fixes and ranking

**Lane:** Engine | **Stage:** Beat 2: Fix it | **Size:** M | **Needs first:** E5 | **Branch:** `e6-candidates`

## The feature

Candidates A, C, D and S derived from the opened paths, simulated, then ranked with the fixed rules; the recommended candidate marked.

## Why it matters

The blunt fix failing on screen (A), the honest UNKNOWN (C), and the recommendation all come from here.
